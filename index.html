The Problem

Every DMO Knows AI Is Here.
Almost None Have a Plan For It.

It's not a question of whether to adopt AI anymore — boards and partners are already asking. The real questions are how, when, and with what budget: who owns it, what the first move actually looks like, and how to fund it without carving out a full-time hire.

No One Owns It

AI strategy falls to whoever's already stretched thinnest — if it's owned at all. There's no time to track a landscape that changes monthly, let alone build a plan around it.

No Clear Starting Point

Staff experiment individually with ChatGPT or Copilot with no shared workflow, no governance, and no sense of what doing this right actually looks like for a DMO.

No Budget Line For It

AI wasn't in last year's budget, and nobody's sure if the right move is a small pilot or a full platform commitment — so it waits for a cycle that never comes.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is the consulting just a way to sell me your CRM? +

No. Consulting is the main business and it stands on its own: engagements never require destnCRM, and most of what we do — governance, training, workflow automation, AI visibility — has nothing to do with it. When you ask us what tool to use, you get a straight answer, including when that answer is a competitor's product or the platform you already run.

Who pays for the AI tools we use? +

You do, directly with the vendor. destnAI is granted delegated administrator access inside your own accounts, and we don't resell or mark up third-party tools. The one thing you could ever buy from us is destnCRM, and that is always a separate decision from a consulting engagement.

When can we get destnCRM? +

It's in active development and not generally available yet. We're taking on a small number of pilot destinations to pressure-test it against real partner data and real migrations before a wider release. If that sounds like your organization, get in touch and we'll walk you through where it stands.

How long does an Assessment take? +

Typically 3–5 weeks from kickoff to a delivered scorecard, findings report, and 90-day roadmap.

Are you an SEO or GEO agency? +

No. SEO and GEO agencies focus on content and search tactics. AI visibility is one of our six service pillars, but most of our work is internal — governance, staff training, workflow automation, and hands-on tool administration across your whole organization. We're a consulting firm for organization-wide AI enablement, functioning as your destination's AI team the way a managed service provider handles your network.
